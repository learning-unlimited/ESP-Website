Emergency Contact

In the unlikely event of an emergency, we need to know who we should contact first, to let them know that you're safe (or that you need help). Often, this is just your parents. However, if your parents will be out of town or unavailable, this might be a family friend or nearby relative.

Please either click below to copy your home address and your parents' contact information into this form, or give contact information for someone else that you know and trust.
