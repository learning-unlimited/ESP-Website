{{ program.niceName }} Class Constraints

Use this page to require that students taking one class also take another, or to prevent students from taking two classes together. Constraints are checked when students add or remove classes during first-come-first-served registration; they are not applied by the class lottery or when administrators edit a student's schedule. Create constraints before registration opens, since a student whose schedule already violates a constraint will be unable to add other classes until they fix it.
