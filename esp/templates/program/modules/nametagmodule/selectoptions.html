Printing nametags

  1. This page generates a PDF. Print it at actual size (100%, no "fit to page") for it to fit snugly in nametag holders. If using nametag backs, print double-sided, flipping on the long edge.
  2. You probably want copytech to print this. If so, use their online form to submit the PDF, and be specific, e.g.:
    I am bringing my own blue cardstock paper, please wait until I bring that. Once it is printed, please cut the paper into IDs by making six cuts along the places that make most sense. In addition, please maintain the order (by giving us the piles you cut out as-is). Thank you!
  3. If printing these yourself: to check that fronts and backs line up, you can print a "Printer calibration scale", and if the offset is worse than 0.1" or so, it may be worth correcting below.
